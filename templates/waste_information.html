Know Your Waste Types

Understanding waste helps protect our environment.

Wet Waste

Food waste, vegetables, fruits, flowers and garden waste.

Dry Waste

Paper, cardboard, clothes, wood and other dry materials.

Plastic Waste

Plastic bottles, bags, containers and packaging materials.

Hazardous Waste

Batteries, chemicals, paints, medicines and electronic waste.

Recycling

Separate recyclable waste to reduce pollution and save resources.

Waste Segregation

Always separate wet and dry waste before disposal.
