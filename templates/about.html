Our Mission

The Real Life Waste Management System is developed to help citizens report waste problems quickly and efficiently. The system allows reporters to submit waste complaints with location and images while enabling verifiers to review and resolve them.

Clean Environment

Promoting cleaner surroundings through effective waste management.

Community Participation

Citizens actively participate in maintaining cleanliness.
